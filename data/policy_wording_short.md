# UnderCover Travel Insurance: Policy Wording, short version

All amounts are in US dollars. The policy record shows the plan, the add-ons, the trip dates, the trip cost and the travelers.

## Limits

| Benefit | Economy | Premium | First |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cancellation and Interruption maximum | 1,500 | 5,000 | 10,000 |
| Trip Delay waiting period, hours | 12 | 6 | 3 |
| Trip Delay maximum | 300 | 800 | 1,500 |
| Baggage Delay waiting period, hours | 24 | 12 | 6 |
| Baggage Delay maximum | 100 | 300 | 500 |
| Baggage maximum | 750 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| Per-item limit | 150 | 300 | 500 |
| Passport maximum | 150 | 250 | 400 |
| Emergency Medical maximum | 25,000 | 100,000 | 250,000 |
| Deductible | 100 | 50 | 0 |

The deductible comes off once per claim, for Baggage and for Emergency Medical only.

## Cancellation and Interruption

Covered reasons only: sickness, injury or death of the traveler, a traveling companion or a family member, confirmed in writing by a physician; severe weather or a natural disaster that stops the carrier for 24 hours or more; home or destination made uninhabitable by fire, flood or storm; jury duty or a subpoena; losing a job through no fault of their own after at least a year with the employer. Cancellation pays the prepaid costs that will not be refunded. Interruption pays the unused prepaid costs that will not be refunded plus a one-way economy fare home. Pay up to the trip cost or the plan maximum, whichever is lower, after refunds and credits.

## Trip Delay

The carrier delays departure by at least the waiting period because of a mechanical or crew problem, a canceled service, severe weather or a strike. Pays meals, accommodation, local transport and rebooking fees during the delay, up to the maximum. Needs the receipts and the carrier's confirmation of the delay and its cause.

## Baggage Delay

Checked baggage arrives at least the waiting period after the traveler does. Pays essential clothing and toiletries, up to the maximum. Needs the receipts and the carrier's delayed baggage report. Not on the journey home.

## Baggage

Loss, theft or accidental damage during the trip, up to the maximum. The per-item limit applies to each item, and a pair or a set counts as one item. Anything a carrier, hotel or other party paid is taken off. Theft needs a police report made within 24 hours. Loss or damage by a carrier needs the carrier's report. Accidental damage needs a repair estimate or a statement that it cannot be repaired.

Not covered: belongings left unattended (out of sight and reach in a public place, or in a vehicle unless locked in the trunk or glove compartment out of sight); cash above $100; wear and tear; damage by moths, vermin, insects or pets; the value of data or work on a device.

## Passport

Replacement fees, photos and local travel to the embassy, up to the maximum. Needs the receipts and an account of what happened.

## Emergency Medical

Necessary treatment of a sudden sickness or injury, up to the maximum. Needs the itemized bill and the medical records. Not covered: a pre-existing condition (symptoms, a physician visit, treatment or a medication change for it in the 60 days before the policy was bought) unless the policy has the Pre-Existing Condition Waiver; adventure sports injuries unless the policy has the Adventure Sports add-on; treatment that could wait until home.

## Add-ons

Adventure Sports covers bungee jumping, skydiving, paragliding, scuba below 10 meters, heli-skiing, roped mountaineering and white-water rafting above grade 3, with any required certification and within its limits. Pre-Existing Condition Waiver removes that exclusion. Electronics Plus raises the per-item limit to $1,200 for phones, tablets, laptops, cameras and lenses, e-readers and smartwatches only.

## Never covered

Anything outside the trip dates; events publicly known before the policy was bought; consequential losses such as lost earnings, missed events or penalties; losses while breaking the law or impaired by alcohol or drugs; war, civil unrest and nuclear incidents; false statements or altered documents.

## Payment

1. Each item at the amount claimed or the per-item limit, whichever is lower.
2. Take off what a carrier or another party paid.
3. Take off the deductible where it applies.
4. Pay the result, up to the benefit maximum.

## Claims

File within 90 days of the incident. If a required document is missing, refer the claim. A family member is a spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent or grandchild. A physician is a licensed doctor who is not the traveler, a companion or a family member. A traveling companion is a traveler named on the policy record.

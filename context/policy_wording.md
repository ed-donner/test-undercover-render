# UnderCover Travel Insurance: Policy Wording

Version 2026.1, for policies bought on or after January 1, 2026.

This document explains what your UnderCover policy covers, what it leaves out and how we work out what we pay. Your policy record shows your plan, any add-ons you bought, the dates of your covered trip and the travelers on it. All amounts are in US dollars.

## 1. Your plan

| Benefit | Economy | Premium | First |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trip Cancellation and Interruption, maximum | $1,500 | $5,000 | $10,000 |
| Trip Delay, waiting period | 12 hours | 6 hours | 3 hours |
| Trip Delay, maximum | $300 | $800 | $1,500 |
| Baggage Delay, waiting period | 24 hours | 12 hours | 6 hours |
| Baggage Delay, maximum | $100 | $300 | $500 |
| Baggage and Personal Effects, maximum | $750 | $1,500 | $3,000 |
| Per-item limit | $150 | $300 | $500 |
| Passport and Travel Documents, maximum | $150 | $250 | $400 |
| Emergency Medical, maximum | $25,000 | $100,000 | $250,000 |
| Deductible | $100 | $50 | $0 |

The deductible applies once per claim to Baggage and Personal Effects and to Emergency Medical. It does not apply to the other benefits.

## 2. Trip Cancellation and Interruption

We pay when you have to cancel your trip before it starts, or cut it short, for one of these reasons:

1. Sickness, injury or death of you, a traveling companion or a family member, confirmed in writing by a physician.
2. Severe weather or a natural disaster that stops your carrier's service for 24 hours or more.
3. Your home or your destination is made uninhabitable by fire, flood or storm.
4. You are called for jury duty or served with a court subpoena.
5. You lose your job through no fault of your own after at least one year with the employer.

We do not pay for cancellation or interruption for any other reason.

For a cancellation we pay the prepaid trip costs that your suppliers will not refund. For an interruption we pay the unused prepaid costs that will not be refunded, plus a one-way economy fare home. We pay up to the trip cost on your policy record or your plan's maximum, whichever is lower. Refunds and credits from suppliers are taken off first.

## 3. Trip Delay

We pay when your carrier delays you for at least your plan's waiting period because of a mechanical or crew problem, a canceled service, severe weather or a strike. The delay runs from the scheduled departure time to the time you actually depart.

We pay reasonable extra costs for meals, accommodation, local transport and rebooking fees during the delay, up to your plan's maximum. Send us the receipts and the carrier's confirmation of the delay and its cause. If a delay on your way home keeps you traveling past your return date, your cover continues until you get home.

## 4. Baggage Delay

We pay when your carrier delivers your checked baggage at least your plan's waiting period after you arrive at your destination. We pay for essential clothing and toiletries bought while you wait, up to your plan's maximum. Send us the receipts and the carrier's delayed baggage report. This benefit does not apply on your journey home.

## 5. Baggage and Personal Effects

We pay for loss, theft or accidental damage to your baggage and personal belongings during your covered trip, up to your plan's maximum.

- The per-item limit applies to every item. A pair or a set, such as a pair of shoes, a suit or a camera with its lens, counts as one item.
- Your carrier pays first. Anything a carrier, hotel or other party pays you for the same loss is taken off what we pay.
- Theft must be reported to the local police within 24 hours, and we need a copy of the police report.
- Loss or damage by a carrier must be reported to the carrier, and we need a copy of its report.
- For accidental damage we need a repair estimate or a statement that the item cannot be repaired.

We do not pay for:

- Belongings left unattended. Unattended means out of your sight and out of your reach in a place the public can get to. Belongings in a vehicle are unattended unless they are locked in the trunk or the glove compartment and out of sight.
- Cash above $100.
- Wear and tear, or gradual deterioration.
- Damage caused by moths, vermin, insects or pets.
- The value of information. For a laptop, a drive, a notebook or a document we pay for the physical item, and we do not pay for the data, the writing or the work it holds.

## 6. Passport and Travel Documents

If your passport or visa is lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed during your covered trip, we pay the replacement fees, the photos, and reasonable local travel to the embassy or consulate, up to your plan's maximum. Send us the receipts and a short account of what happened.

## 7. Emergency Medical

We pay for necessary treatment of a sudden sickness or injury during your covered trip, up to your plan's maximum. Send us the itemized bill and the medical records from the visit.

We do not pay for:

- Pre-existing conditions. A condition is pre-existing if, in the 60 days before you bought the policy, you had symptoms of it, saw a physician about it, received treatment for it or had a change in medication for it. The Pre-Existing Condition Waiver removes this exclusion.
- Injuries from adventure sports, unless you bought the Adventure Sports add-on.
- Treatment that can reasonably wait until you are home.

## 8. Add-ons

**Adventure Sports.** Adventure sports are bungee jumping, skydiving, paragliding, scuba diving deeper than 10 meters (33 feet), heli-skiing, roped mountaineering and white-water rafting above grade 3. With this add-on we cover injuries from these sports, provided you hold any certification the activity requires and stay within its limits.

**Pre-Existing Condition Waiver.** With this add-on the pre-existing condition exclusion in section 7 does not apply.

**Electronics Plus.** With this add-on the per-item limit for electronics is $1,200. Electronics are phones, tablets, laptops, cameras and their lenses, e-readers and smartwatches.

## 9. General exclusions

No benefit pays for:

- Anything that happens outside the dates of your covered trip.
- Events that were publicly known before you bought the policy, such as a named storm or an announced strike.
- Consequential losses, which are losses that follow indirectly from an incident, such as lost earnings, missed events or contractual penalties.
- Losses while you are breaking the law or impaired by alcohol or drugs.
- War, civil unrest and nuclear incidents.
- Any claim supported by false statements or altered documents.

## 10. How we work out your payment

1. We start with the eligible amount of each item or expense. For belongings that is the amount claimed or the per-item limit, whichever is lower.
2. We take off anything a carrier or another party has paid you for the same loss.
3. We take off the deductible, where it applies.
4. We pay the result, up to the maximum for the benefit.

## 11. How to claim

File your claim within 90 days of the incident. Tell us what happened in your own words, list what you are claiming and attach the documents named in the section for your benefit. If a document is missing we will ask you for it, and the claim waits until it arrives.

## 12. Definitions

- **Carrier:** an airline, railway, ferry, cruise or coach company that you have a ticket with.
- **Covered trip:** the trip on your policy record, from the departure date to the return date.
- **Family member:** your spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent or grandchild.
- **Physician:** a licensed medical doctor who is not you, a traveling companion or a family member.
- **Traveling companion:** a person named as a traveler on your policy record.
